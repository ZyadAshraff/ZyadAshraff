## Featured Projects

### 🛍️ [SmartSales Bot: AI Shopping Assistant](https://github.com/ZyadAshraff/Intelligent-Sales-ChatBot)
**NLP · RAG · LLMs**

A conversational shopping assistant that understands customer queries and retrieves the right products from a large catalog.

- **Hybrid retrieval:** semantic search with **FAISS** combined with keyword-based **BM25**
- Conversational memory, **intent and sentiment analysis**, and personalized recommendations
- LLM response generation via **OpenRouter** with fallback handling, plus deterministic business logic for pricing, categories, and installment calculations
- Multilingual support and a Streamlit front end

`Python` `FAISS` `BM25` `XLM-R` `OpenRouter` `Streamlit`

---

### 📈 [DeepMetrix: Retail Sales Forecasting](https://github.com/ZyadAshraff/DeepMetrix)
**Time Series · Gradient Boosting · Deployed**

Demand forecasting on the **M5 dataset**, served through a web app on **Hugging Face Spaces**.

- **LightGBM** and **XGBoost** models with rolling-window features, proper categorical handling, and Tweedie objectives
- Per-store training loops for both model families
- Interactive UI with grouped inputs and confidence indicators

`LightGBM` `XGBoost` `Pandas` `Hugging Face Spaces`

---

### 🏃 [Human Activity Recognition (UCI HAR)](https://github.com/ZyadAshraff/Human-Activity-Recognition---UCI-HAR-Dataset)
**Machine Learning · Deep Learning · Transformers**

A multi-phase system classifying **6 activities** from accelerometer and gyroscope signals (561 features, 10K+ samples).

- Benchmarked **8 models**: KNN, Naïve Bayes, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, 1D-CNN, Vanilla RNN, GRU, and LSTM
- Fine-tuned IBM's **PatchTST** Transformer (Hugging Face) on raw sensor sequences with subject-based splits
- Evaluated on accuracy, weighted F1, per-class F1, training time, and full confusion-matrix analysis

`PyTorch` `TensorFlow` `scikit-learn` `Hugging Face`

---

### 🧪 [Disease Prediction with Hyperparameter Optimization]([https://github.com/ZyadAshraff/Disease-Prediction](https://github.com/ZyadAshraff/Model-Hyperparameter-Optimization))
**Neural Networks from Scratch**

A comparative study of from-scratch vs. framework-based training on a binary medical image classification task.

- Implemented **forward/backpropagation, 5 optimizers, and custom Grid & Random Search** with no external ML libraries
- Studied the effect of depth (1–5 hidden layers), activation functions, and optimizer choice on validation accuracy
- Produced structured result logs and performance visualizations

`Python` `NumPy`
